How it works

From upload to published in four steps.

1

Sign in

Create an account and open the dashboard.

2

Connect your accounts

Authorize TikTok, YouTube or Instagram through each platform's official sign-in screen.

3

Add video and title

Upload your video, write the title and choose the date, time and platforms.

4

Schedule

Confirm the schedule. Evrix Social publishes the video at the chosen time.

How Evrix Social works with TikTok

Evrix Social lets TikTok creators schedule their own videos. We connect to your account only after you authorize it on TikTok's official authorization screen, and we use that access only to publish the content you upload and schedule.

  • You sign in with TikTok through the official authorization flow. We never ask for your TikTok password.
  • We only publish videos that you uploaded and scheduled yourself, at the date and time you chose.
  • You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, and you can also revoke access from your TikTok settings.
  • Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the features described above. See our Privacy Policy for details.
